Client-Side Architecture & Data Safety Notice

Why your data is private and safe:

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Every invoice, quotation, line item, bank detail, and customer address remains exclusively inside your browser's local memory.
  • No Backend Database: There is no remote cloud server receiving your commercial figures, client names, GSTIN/tax IDs, or financial ledgers.
  • Works 100% Offline: You can disconnect your internet, turn on Airplane Mode, and create or print invoices without network access.
  • Zero Trackers or Telemetry: No analytics cookies, no surveillance beacons, and no account signups required.

Important: Understanding the risk of Browser Storage (IndexedDB)

Because your records are saved locally in your browser's IndexedDB and not on an external cloud server, your data can be wiped if:

  • You clear your browser cache, history, or cookies (via "Clear Browsing Data").
  • You use an Incognito / Private Browsing window (which purges storage when the tab or window closes).
  • A disk cleanup utility, privacy cleaner, or operating system optimizer cleans up browser site storage.
  • You switch browser profiles, reset your computer, or change devices.
Best Practice: Export regular JSON backups

Download a 1-click JSON backup file at the end of each billing cycle to ensure your records are permanently safe.

InvoiceCraft Quick Start & User Guide

A fast overview of tools, workflows, and keyboard shortcuts

Quick-Start Workflow

1. Type & Model

Choose Invoice, Quotation, Estimate, or Proforma. Select from 10 Swiss, Minimalist, or Corporate architectural layouts.

2. Brand & Details

Open Logo Studio to upload a logo, craft a custom monogram, or pick vector icons. Set dates, currency, & comma styles.

3. Items & Taxes

Set individual tax rates per item (0% Exempt, 5%, 12%, 18% GST). Configure official multi-leg splits (e.g. 50% CGST + 50% SGST).

Key Capabilities

Continuous Numbering: Click the Numbering Engine button next to Invoice # to configure auto-incrementing patterns ({PREFIX}/{FY}/{COUNTER}), starting counter offsets (e.g. resume from 576), and year resets. Use Next Seq # for a one-click sequential refresh.
Comma Formatting: Toggle between International (1,234,567.89) and Indian Lakhs/Crores (12,34,567.89) in Module 3.
Multiple Companies: Manage 3, 5, or 10+ business entities. Switch companies with 1 click using the top navbar or sender module.
Quotation Conversion: Convert approved Estimates or Quotations into final Tax Invoices with a single click.
Bank & Tax Guard: If you leave Bank Details or Tax ID blank, InvoiceCraft automatically optimizes the layout to cleanly omit empty boxes.
Print & Vector PDF Export

Click Print / Save PDF (or press Ctrl+P) to export:

  • Destination: Save as PDF (generates a 100% selectable, crystal-sharp vector PDF)
  • Optimized layout guarantees a clean 1-page fit with zero browser headers or URL watermarks.
Keyboard Shortcuts: Ctrl+B Toggle Sidebar Ctrl+S Save to IndexedDB Ctrl+P Print / Save PDF
