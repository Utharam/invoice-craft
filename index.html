Your Data is 100% Private

Client-Side Architecture & Data Safety Notice

Why your data is private and safe:

  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Every invoice, quotation, line item, bank detail, and customer address remains exclusively inside your browser's local memory.
  • No Backend Database: There is no remote cloud server receiving your commercial figures, client names, GSTIN/tax IDs, or financial ledgers.
  • Works 100% Offline: You can disconnect your internet, turn on Airplane Mode, and create or print invoices without network access.
  • Zero Trackers or Telemetry: No analytics cookies, no surveillance beacons, and no account signups required.

Important: Understanding the risk of Browser Storage (IndexedDB)

Because your records are saved locally in your browser's IndexedDB and not on an external cloud server, your data can be wiped if:

  • You clear your browser cache, history, or cookies (via "Clear Browsing Data").
  • You use an Incognito / Private Browsing window (which purges storage when the tab or window closes).
  • A disk cleanup utility, privacy cleaner, or operating system optimizer cleans up browser site storage.
  • You switch browser profiles, reset your computer, or change devices.
Best Practice: Export regular JSON backups

Download a 1-click JSON backup file at the end of each billing cycle to ensure your records are permanently safe.

InvoiceCraft 1.3.0

A quiet workspace for invoices, quotes, and the records behind them.

InvoiceCraft runs entirely in your browser. Documents, clients, companies, saved items, and numbering settings stay in local IndexedDB. There is no account, server, sync, or telemetry. Keep a JSON backup, and your records travel with you.

Getting started

Home is the default screen. It shows your active draft, quick actions, recent documents, and a compact per-currency snapshot. Compose is the editor; Reports and Settings are one click away in the header.

  1. 01

    Open or continue a document

    Use Continue draft to resume the last autosaved document, or New Invoice to begin. The More menu holds guide, companies, previous invoices, clients, backup, and privacy.

  2. 02

    Work through the six modules

    The left sidebar is ordered: type and model, brand, details, parties, items and taxes, then payment and notes. The right canvas stays in sync as you type.

  3. 03

    Save, print, or export

    Save and Print / Save PDF sit in the Compose toolbar above the canvas. Saving a document stores a frozen record in IndexedDB; printing uses the browser’s native vector PDF output.

Compose stays mounted while you move between Home, Reports, and Settings, so returning to it does not discard your work. Autosave runs as you type, but only Save adds a document to history.

Documents & numbering

InvoiceCraft supports four document types: Invoice, Quotation, Estimate, and Proforma Invoice. Each has its own status language, defaults, and placement in reports.

Independent sequences

Every document type owns its own counter. A quotation does not consume an invoice number, and a fresh invoice does not move the quotation sequence. Open Settings > Numbering to edit the four counters, preview the next number, choose a pattern such as {PREFIX}/{FY}/{COUNTER}, and set the financial-year reset.

Saving the same document again does not consume another number. Manual mode never generates or advances numbers automatically. Saved documents are never renumbered by changing a numbering setting.

Converting a quote

Convert an approved quotation, estimate, or proforma into an invoice from the editor. The original source remains in history with a link, and the new invoice takes the next invoice number. A source can be converted once.

A number is part of the document record. If you need a different type, start a new document; a saved record is never rewritten in place.

The sheet uses one shared calculation for line totals, tax, tax-leg allocation, and amount in words. A tax leg is a share of the total tax, not a second tax rate. Missing item tax rates default to 18%.

Saved items

Module 5 includes a small item library so common services and products do not need to be retyped. The picker shows Global items first, then items for the active company, both in one search and filtered to the document’s currency.

Reuse an item

Focus the Saved items search, choose a result, and InvoiceCraft adds a line with quantity 1, the saved price, tax, and HSN. Adjust quantity or discount on the line as usual.

Save an item

Each line item has a bookmark action. It saves or updates the description, HSN, price, tax, and currency. The default save scope is set in Settings > Item library: Shared across the app or Per company.

  • Global items are available in every document.
  • Company items appear only when that company is linked to the document.
  • Matching scope, company, currency, and description update the existing item instead of creating a duplicate.
  • Editing the library never changes a saved invoice; the invoice keeps its own line-item snapshot.

The library is currency-aware. A USD item will not appear in an INR document at the wrong price. Switch the document currency in Module 3 to see the matching items.

Clients & companies

Clients in the More menu stores names, emails, phones, addresses, and tax IDs. Each client card shows invoice totals per currency, a document count, and an expandable history. Load a past document, or start a fresh one with the client prefilled.

Companies stores sender profiles. Each company can carry a default currency and number format for new documents. Applying a company to an existing document asks before changing its currency; hand-editing the sender fields detaches the document from that company so reports stay honest.

History & reports

Previous invoices

Open More > Previous invoices for the local document archive. Search by number, client, email, or line-item description; filter by document type or client; then load, duplicate, or delete a record. Conversion remarks appear on both the source and the invoice it became.

Reports

Reports has three views: Dashboard, Documents, and Tax. Filter by period, company, client, document type, status, and currency. Each currency is reported separately; values are never added across currencies.

  • Dashboard shows document counts by type and status, invoice revenue, paid and outstanding amounts, open quotes, paid proformas, conversion counts, and tax.
  • Documents is a filterable register with load, CSV export, and conversion remarks.
  • Tax groups tax by item rate and by tax-leg share for the selected period.

Revenue means invoices only. Quotes, estimates, and proformas are pipeline; drafts are excluded from financial totals. Use the report print button for a clean printed report.

Backup & privacy

Everything is local. There is no cloud account and no analytics. Clearing browser site data removes your records, so treat backups as part of the workflow.

  • Backup in the More menu exports a JSON file containing documents, clients, companies, numbering, the active draft, saved items, and the item-library scope.
  • Backups are version 4. Older version 2 and 3 files remain importable.
  • Restore overwrites records with the same ID and keeps other local records.
  • Privacy in the More menu explains local storage, storage risks, and backup recommendations.

Keep a recent backup on a separate drive. Browser storage can be cleared by privacy tools, browser cleanup, or site-data deletion.

Printing & shortcuts

In Compose, use Print / Save PDF in the canvas toolbar and choose Save as PDF. The print stylesheet produces vector text, a clean A4 page, and no browser header or footer marks. The page badge above the canvas shows the estimated A4 length; click it to toggle boundary guides.

ShortcutAction
Ctrl + PPrint the active view: invoice in Compose, report in Reports.
Ctrl + SSave the document in Compose, even while typing in a field. Ignored in dialogs and other views.
Ctrl + BToggle the Compose sidebar when no field or dialog is active.

On macOS, use Cmd instead of Ctrl. Reports print the report layout only; Home and Settings do not print.
